# Code of conduct

Treat other participants with respect. Critique ideas and results with specific evidence. Make
room for different experience levels and for people who communicate in a second language.
Respect privacy: redact identities, credentials, local paths and private research artifacts.

Harassment, threats, slurs, unwanted sexual attention, publishing someone's private information,
and retaliation for raising a concern are unacceptable in project spaces.

Ask a maintainer for a private reporting channel if none is listed. Do not post sensitive details
in a public issue. Maintainers should limit disclosure, hear the people involved where safe,
and respond proportionately. Responses may include removing a post, a warning, restricting
participation, or banning an account. The same expectations apply to maintainers.
